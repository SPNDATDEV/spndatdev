# 👋 We're SPNDATDEV

### Full-Stack Development Team • Builders • Music Technology Innovators

We build **full-stack applications and bring digital products from idea to deployment.**

Specializing in **music technology, Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) platforms, e-commerce, AI integrations, and digital ecosystems** that empower artists, brands, and businesses.

---

# 🚀 Projects

Building the future of music technology, artist independence, and Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) commerce.

1. 🎵 **SPNDAT** — Multiplatform music, social, and entertainment ecosystem featuring music discovery, streaming, artist engagement, and digital monetization.

2. 🌐 **SPNDAT Co.** — Corporate website and technology hub showcasing our music platforms, digital services, DTC solutions, and development ecosystem.

3. 🎤 **Street Flavor Records** — Independent record label platform supporting music releases, artist development, merchandise, and direct-to-fan commerce.

4. 🛒 **Powered by SPNDAT** — DTC commerce and marketing infrastructure providing custom artist storefronts, merchandise solutions, digital distribution integrations, payment processing, and direct fan engagement.

5. 🔗 **The DAT Chain** — Connected DTC artist and brand directory powered by SPNDAT, bringing together artist storefronts, merchandise, music, and exclusive products into one discoverable ecosystem.

6. 🎧 **Artist DTC Platforms** — Custom-built, artist-branded storefronts supporting exclusive merchandise, physical music, digital products, and direct-to-fan experiences.

   **Projects include:**
   - Remy Ma
   - Young Buck
   - Struggle Jennings
   - Mathematics
